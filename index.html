Regional Overview

A New Region Awaits

Welcome to Nubalia, a Pokémon adventure shaped by exploration, discovery, and the challenge of becoming a Champion.

Travel through diverse environments, encounter Pokémon from multiple generations, and face trainers who stand between you and your next milestone. Nubalia is being developed as a complete regional journey, with a progression system built around routes, increasingly challenging battles, and 18 specialized Gyms.

Evolving Beta Journey Nubalia is an actively evolving project. This stable beta provides the initial foundation for the region. Progression, encounter tables, trainer balance, and Gym implementations will continuously expand in future updates.

Intended Regional Distribution Strategy

To craft a distinctive regional ecosystem, Nubalia emphasizes specific generations in standard route encounter tables:

Primary Focus

Gens IV, V & VI

Sinnoh, Unova, and Kalos species form the core encounters of the region.

Secondary Focus

Gens III & VII

Hoenn and Alola Pokémon enrich specialized habitats and mountain peaks.

Specialized / Rare

Gens I & II

Kanto and Johto Pokémon appear less commonly in ordinary trainer rosters.

Biomes O' Plenty

Vast array of stunning custom biomes providing unique backdrop landscapes.

When Dungeons Arise

Massive dungeons, Seven Seas temples, and rogue structures to explore.

Waystones & Compasses

Nature's & Explorer's Compasses for streamlined world travel across Nubalia.

Farmer's Delight & Chipped

Expanded culinary crafting, building blocks, and architectural customization.
